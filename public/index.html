Questions

Good to know

Do students need an account?

No. They enter the class code and a display name. Nothing else.

How many people can join?

Up to 12 per class. Video works best with about 6–8 cameras on, so ask larger groups to keep cameras off unless speaking.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in any modern browser. Drawing works with touch and stylus. Screen sharing is limited on some phones.

Is recording private?

Only the teacher can record, the file is saved on the teacher's own device, and everyone sees a "Recording" badge while it runs.

Can I use the course without a live class?

Yes. Open Courses, pick a lesson and work through the practice questions. Progress is saved in your browser.
